Volunteer in Coconut Grove · Food for Life

A Little of Your Time. A Lot of Heart.

Some of the most meaningful connections happen when we show up for one another. Through Food for Life, we bring the spirit of Seva into our community, sharing nourishment, kindness, and care with those around us.

Join Us in Giving Back →

The Meaning of Seva

Love in motion.

In Sanskrit, seva means service offered without expectation of reward or recognition. It is the yoga of action: showing up for something beyond yourself.

We believe yoga practiced only on the mat is incomplete. Seva is how the attention we cultivate in practice finds its way into the kitchen, onto the street, and into the way we care for our corner of Miami. You don't need any experience or background in the tradition. You only need to be willing to help.

"The real test is what you carry out the door."

Ways to Get Involved

Find the way you'd like to help.

The kitchen is always open to helpers. Choose what calls to you, and we'll be in touch about how to begin.

i.

Prepare

Help create nourishing meals.

Chop, cook, and lend a hand in a kitchen that runs on devotion. Every meal is made from scratch with organic, vegetarian ingredients.

ii.

Serve

Help share meals with the community.

Help serve the hot meal after class, or join the volunteers who bring free meals to neighbors in downtown Miami.

iii.

Connect

Build meaningful relationships through shared service.

Wash dishes, help set the table, and get to know the people you serve alongside. Shared work has a way of turning strangers into friends.

Our Community Impact

200

About 200 neighbors nourished every week.

Each one is a person, not a number: someone who stayed after class, someone who found a warm plate downtown on a Monday. No questions asked, no registration required. Just food, made with love, offered freely.

  • SundaysAbout 100 people share a meal at our center for a small donation.
  • MondaysAbout 100 free meals are served to unhoused neighbors in downtown Miami.
