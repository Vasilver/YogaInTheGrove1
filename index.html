What We Offer

Three ways in. One practice.

Yoga in the Grove is a neighborhood studio on Virginia Street, a place to breathe, move, chant, and belong. We offer Hatha Asana, Kirtan, and community gatherings where everyone is welcome, exactly as they are.

Our practice is rooted in two traditions: Hatha, the yoga of the body, and Bhakti, the yoga of devotion. Together, they remind us that yoga is about more than movement. It's about connection, community, and union.

i.

Hatha Asana

Breath, alignment, steadiness.

+

A traditional yoga practice centered on mindful movement, breath, and alignment. Whether you're stepping onto the mat for the first time or deepening an existing practice, there's a place for you here. We meet you exactly where you are, every time.

ii.

Kirtan

Where connections grow.

+

Kirtan is a shared practice of call-and-response chanting, rooted in devotion, connection, and community. It's your space to slow down, open your heart, and experience the joy of coming together. No experience is necessary. Whether you join in or simply listen, you're part of something meaningful.

See kirtan dates →About the practice →
iii.

Seva

Practice beyond the mat.

+

Seva is the practice of selfless service, rooted in kindness, compassion, and connection. It's about showing up for one another and giving back to the community we call home. Through small acts of service and shared purpose, we bring the spirit of yoga into everyday life.

Our Community Initiatives: Morning Seva in Coconut Grove and Food for Life meal deliveries.

The Community

Come for the practice. Stay for the people.

This is not a fitness studio with a loyalty program. It's a gathering place: a living room, a porch, a circle of regulars who leave the door open for anyone new. We've had beginners and longtime practitioners sit side by side for years, and that's exactly how we like it. Whatever brought you here, you belong in the room.

Your First Visit

Come As You Are. We'll Take It From Here.

New to yoga? New to our community? You're welcome exactly as you are. Here's everything you need to know before joining us on the mat.

"If you can breathe, you can practice."

Do I need any yoga experience?

Not at all. Whether it's your very first class or you've been practicing for years, there's a place for you here. Our teachers meet you where you are, helping you move, breathe, and grow at your own pace.

Does it cost anything?

No. Every class, Kirtan evening and gathering at Yoga in the Grove is free, always. If you'd like to give back, you can support our community meals.

What is Kirtan?

Kirtan is a shared practice of call-and-response chanting that brings people together through sound, devotion, and connection. You don't need to know the words or have any musical experience. Simply come as you are, listen, and join in whenever you feel ready.

Do I need to sign up before coming?

We recommend checking our schedule before your visit to find a class or gathering that speaks to you. You can explore upcoming sessions and any registration details on our calendar. Explore the Schedule →

Is there food?

Yes. After every Kirtan evening we share a free vegetarian meal, called prasadam, prepared with care and offered in a spirit of generosity. Everyone is welcome to stay and eat with us.

Where can I park?

Park on Day Street, behind the white fence. Walk around the building on your left and you're at the studio, 3220 Virginia Street in Coconut Grove. Get Directions →

Can I get here by public transportation?

Yes. The studio is a short walk from the Coconut Grove Metrorail station, and a five-minute ride from Brickell. Plan Your Visit →
